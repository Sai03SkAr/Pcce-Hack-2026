1

Project summary

The problem

India's family-owned traders, distributors and multi-branch shops run on WhatsApp groups, Excel sheets, a paper or Khatabook udhaar register, and an accountant who keys everything into Tally weeks later. The owner has no single, current view of how the business and each branch is doing. Stock runs out at one branch while another has too much. Udhaar gets old without anyone noticing. Goods expire on the shelf. Tally needs accounting knowledge that a 10th or 12th-pass owner or staff member does not have.

Our solution

Pedhi (पेढी, the Marathi and Gujarati word for a family firm) is one app for the whole family business:

Runs the counter

  • Billing by tap, search or voice ("Ramesh ko 10 bori ACC cement udhaar")
  • Stock per branch, transfers, purchases, returns
  • Udhaar khata with reminders and UPI links
  • Staff can also work entirely from WhatsApp

Keeps the books

  • Proper double-entry accounts the way Tally keeps them, built from plain questions ("Money came in", "Bought stock")
  • GST (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B), P&L, Balance Sheet
  • Tally XML export and a CA login with sign-off

Thinks for the owner

  • A 7 am brief on WhatsApp: yesterday, problems ranked by rupees at stake, one-tap fixes
  • Ask Pedhi in Hinglish, answered from real data
  • Stock plan: what to order, what's expiring, which items earn

Stays trustworthy

  • The AI never calculates a number; code does
  • WhatsApp entries are saved only after the sender replies YES
  • Role walls, credit limits, audit log, period lock

Who it is for

Retailers, distributors, wholesalers and family enterprises with 1 to 10 branches, where the owner and family need oversight and the counter staff need something as simple as WhatsApp.

Our angle. Most projects in this space are single-shop udhaar or voice-billing apps. Pedhi targets the multi-branch family business. It gives a consolidated view across branches, cross-branch intelligence (move stock instead of buying it), and real accounts the CA can import into Tally, so the owner needs no other app.

One line for the judges

"Staff bill at the counter or on WhatsApp. Every morning the owner gets one message that says what happened, what is wrong and what to do about it, across every branch. The books are already done for the CA."

2

The big picture: how it flows

Everything enters through one rules layer, lands in one database, is analysed by rule engines, and comes out as screens, WhatsApp messages and reports. The AI only words things and picks which tool to run.

A bill lowers stock at that branch, adds to the customer's udhaar if on credit, and posts a Sales voucher with CGST and SGST. All of this happens in one transaction, and Undo reverses all of it.
A purchase raises stock, records GST input credit, records what you owe the supplier and stores each line's use-by date.
Every morning the insight engine re-ranks problems by rupees at stake. The brief is built from those facts and sent to each family member.

WhatsApp: approve before save

Staff already text all day. Pedhi lets them run the counter from WhatsApp with no training, and the doc's key trust rule holds: the AI proposes, a person approves. Nothing reaches the books until the sender replies YES. NO cancels. A proposal goes stale after 30 minutes.

Every feature, module by module

Today (owner, family, manager) core

  • Morning brief as it arrives on WhatsApp; Hindi / English toggle
  • KPIs: today, last 7 days vs last week, margin, udhaar, stock value
  • Issues ranked by ₹ at stake with one-tap fixes (transfer, remind, open)
  • 30-day sales by branch chart, top sellers, branch cards

New bill (everyone) core

  • Search, tap tiles, or say the whole bill by voice
  • Cash / UPI / udhaar; stock and credit-limit checks
  • Add a new customer inline; GST tax invoice (CGST/SGST or IGST)
  • Phone-friendly cart; staff price edits ignored

Stock and Stock plan new

  • Every item across every branch side by side, with days of stock left
  • Transfers, corrections with reason, new items
  • Order now, grouped by supplier, with a WhatsApp order draft
  • Expiring in 30 days from use-by dates on bills
  • What sells, what earns: Stars, Crowd-pullers, Push it, Not moving; demand trend

Udhaar khata replaces Khatabook

  • Outstanding with ageing; per-customer credit days and limit
  • Got money / gave money / charge / discount / write-off
  • Old balances from a paper khata; customer import
  • Private statement link with a UPI pay button
  • Automatic WhatsApp reminders; suppliers khata

Books replaces Tally

  • Plain-language entries: Money came in, Bought stock, Paid supplier, Expense, Moved money, Owner money, or "Tell Pedhi"
  • All 8 Tally voucher types incl. credit/debit notes and journal
  • Day Book, Ledgers, Trial Balance, P&L (also per branch), Balance Sheet
  • Bank reconciliation, backup and restore

GST and CA compliance

  • GSTR-3B summary; GSTR-1 B2B, B2C by rate, HSN, credit notes
  • IGST when the customer's state differs (from GSTIN)
  • Tally XML (masters + vouchers) and CSVs
  • Read-only CA login; CA sign-off locks the period

Ask Pedhi AI

  • English, Hindi or Hinglish, typed or spoken
  • 11 typed data tools; every answer shows "how this was calculated"
  • Expiry, what to order, demand, udhaar, branches, margins

Also included

  • Day close: count the drawer, shortages booked and flagged
  • Import sheet: messy Excel columns mapped automatically
  • Help: in-app guide answering "how do I…" in Hinglish
  • Activity: who did what, when, from which channel
  • Installable app (PWA) on Android and iPhone; Live refresh

6

How the AI is kept honest

Numbers come from code

The metrics layer computes every figure. The insight engine finds and sizes problems with plain rules. The model only rewrites the brief's wording, told to copy every number exactly.

Tools, not SQL

Ask Pedhi chooses from 11 typed tools. Our code runs them, enforces the user's branch and strips money fields from staff results before the model sees them.

Approval before writing

Chat tools are read-only. A WhatsApp message is only a proposal until the sender replies YES. New items need prices from a manager.

Works without AI

With no API key, every feature runs on the rule engine, so the demo can't break if the model or the Wi-Fi fails.
Accounting rules built in: every voucher balances (debits = credits); stock is valued at cost excluding GST; udhaar is applied oldest bill first; nothing is deleted, corrections are reversing entries; a CA-locked period can't be changed.

Demo story and 5-minute walkthrough

The seed creates 90 days of real-looking history for Naik Traders (building materials, 3 branches in Goa), with stories planted for the app to find:

1Mapusa sales are down ~30% because its best-selling ACC cement ran out, while Ponda has 46 days of it → Transfer suggested
2Three contractors owe ₹1.76 lakh, 46–49 days old, one over his limit → Send reminders with UPI links
3₹62k of premium paint has not sold in 80+ days (dead stock)
4Cash was short at Ponda on three Tuesdays, same person closing
5One counter keeps billing a TMT rod below cost
6A regular contractor hasn't ordered in 25 days
7White cement at Ponda expires in 6 days; wall putty at Mapusa in 19
8Good news: Margao's best day in three months (Dhanteras)

Walkthrough

  1. Sign in as Suresh (Owner). Read the brief, switch to हिं. Tap Transfer from Ponda.
  2. WhatsApp as Raju: "Dhavalikar ko 10 bori ACC cement udhaar" → bill shown → "haan" → saved. Show it in Sales and the udhaar.
  3. As Rohan: "20 bag Dalmia cement aaya exp 02/2027" → "add this item?" → "YES 395 355" → "yes". Keep Stock plan open on a second phone: it updates by itself.
  4. Stock plan: what to order (WhatsApp draft to the supplier), what's expiring, the Stars.
  5. Udhaar → Remind all overdue → message with UPI link; share a customer's statement.
  6. Ask Pedhi: "Is hafte kaunsi branch ka margin sabse kam hai?" → open "How this was calculated".
  7. Sign in as Raju: no cost prices anywhere; an over-limit udhaar bill is blocked.
  8. Books: "Goa Cement se 200 bag ACC cement liya udhaar" → voucher → P&L and Balance Sheet match → GST.
  9. As CA Prakash: CA pack → Tally XML → Sign off and lock.
Before the pitch: owner → Reset demo data. Keep a screen recording as backup. The app runs offline on the laptop; only phones (tunnel) and the AI key need internet.
