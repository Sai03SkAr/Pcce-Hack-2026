Pedhi

The AI munim for family businesses

Sales · Stock · Udhaar · Books and GST · WhatsApp. One app, every branch.

Pedhi is the Marathi and Gujarati word for a family firm. We built the munim every family business wishes it had: one that keeps the books, watches the stock and talks to the owner on WhatsApp.
