The problem

Today, a family business lives in four places

WhatsApp groups

Orders, payments and “send 20 bags” arrive as chats. None of it becomes a record.

A paper khata

Who owes what, and since when, sits in one notebook at one counter.

Excel per branch

Each branch counts its own stock. Nobody sees all branches at once.

Tally, weeks later

An accountant types it in at month end. Most owners can’t use it themselves.

So the owner never has one current view of the business, or of each branch.

This is the problem statement in the owner’s words. The data exists, but it is scattered, late and locked in tools only an accountant can use.
