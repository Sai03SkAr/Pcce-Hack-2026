Who it is for

One business, four kinds of people

Retailers, distributors and wholesalers with one to ten branches, run by a family.

Owner and family

See everything

Every branch, profit and udhaar. The 7 am brief on WhatsApp.

Branch manager

Runs one branch

Stock, bills, transfers and deliveries for that branch only.

Counter staff

Bills fast

By tap, voice or WhatsApp. Never sees cost prices or profit.

The CA

Signs off

Reads the books and GST, exports to Tally, locks a period.

Each person signs in with their own password and sees a different business. The same rules apply on WhatsApp.
